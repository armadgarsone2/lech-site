Privacy Policy – lech

Effective date: 30 October 2026.

lech is a personal-use tool operated by its owner, for the owner's use only. It is not a public service. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

Data accessed

  • Google Drive: files the owner chooses to upload through the tool, and the metadata needed to complete those uploads (file names, sizes, and folder identifiers). Access is granted by the owner through Google OAuth and is used only to upload files to the owner's own Drive.
  • Telegram: messages the owner sends to their own bot (links and commands) that are needed to carry out a requested download or upload.

How data is used

Data is used solely to perform the operations the owner requests: downloading a requested file and uploading it to the owner's own Google Drive. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no sale of data.

Storage

The Google OAuth token is stored on the owner's own server. Uploaded files are stored in the owner's own Google Drive account. No copies are kept by any third party.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to third parties, except as required to perform the requested operation (uploading to the owner's Drive through Google's API).

Retention and revocation

Uploaded files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke the tool's access to Google Drive at any time from their Google Account permissions page; revocation immediately stops any further access.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the owner through the same Telegram bot used to operate the tool.
