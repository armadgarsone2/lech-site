lech

lech is a personal-use tool that mirrors files its owner requests to the owner's own Google Drive. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

How it works

  1. The owner sends a download link to their private Telegram bot.
  2. The bot downloads the requested file.
  3. The bot uploads the file to the owner's own Google Drive (a mirror) and returns a Drive link.
  4. Nothing is shared with anyone else.

The bot itself runs on the owner's own server. Google Drive is used only as the owner's personal storage.

Privacy Policy

lech is a personal tool operated by its owner for their own use.
